Stonetop Adventures

Adventure sites for Stonetop, written to be run straight off the screen: prep, travel, room-by-room notes and stat blocks on one sheet, with clickable dice, HP trackers and hover previews of the rulebook pages each site draws on. 3 of the 7 have been run at a table; the rest are flagged.
